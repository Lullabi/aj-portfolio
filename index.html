I studied Information Sciences at the University of Tennessee, where I combined UX design with programming. Now I research, design and build the tools engineers use to keep wind and solar sites running.

  1. Listen

    Contextual inquiry and interviews with the people doing the work, at their desks and in their real tools.

  2. Map

    Competitive analysis, flows and wireframes so the team agrees on the problem before anything gets built.

  3. Make

    Designs in Figma that I can take all the way to production in React, so nothing gets lost in handoff.

  4. Test

    Usability sessions with real users, then fixes shipped while the feedback is still fresh.
